Here's a hard truth. Most sales are lost because the seller never asked for the sale. They explain, they charm, and then they just stop.

Why does it happen? Fear. Asking feels pushy, so we wait and hope the customer says yes on their own.

But the customer is waiting too. They want you to guide them to the next step.

Method one: assume the sale. Don't ask if. Ask which. Which color works better for you, black or white?

Method two: the next step. So, shall we get you started today? Simple, clear, and no pressure.

Method three: the silence. After you ask, stop talking. The first person to speak usually gives in.

And if they say no? That's fine. Ask: what would need to change for this to be a yes? Now you've found the real objection.

So at the end of every pitch, pick a close, ask once, and stay quiet.

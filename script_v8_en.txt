The best salespeople don't talk the most. They ask the most. Here are three questions that do the selling for you.

Question one: what made you look into this? It tells you the real reason they're here.

Question two: what have you tried so far? It shows you what already failed, so you don't repeat it.

Question three: what happens if nothing changes? Suddenly, the cost of waiting feels real.

Why does it work? Because people don't argue with their own answers. When they say it out loud, they convince themselves.

Example: a customer says they need a new phone. Ask: what's wrong with the old one? They say: the battery dies by lunch. Now you're not selling a phone. You're selling a full day of battery.

Here's a good rule: let the customer talk sixty percent of the time. Then repeat their own words back to them.

So before your next call, ask why they're here, ask what they've tried, and ask what happens if nothing changes.

A customer says: "That's too expensive." Most sellers panic and drop the price. Don't.

Most of the time, "too expensive" doesn't mean "I have no money." It means: "I don't see the value yet."

Mistake one: cutting the price straight away. It tells them your price was never real.

Mistake two: arguing. The more you defend your price, the more they dig in.

So what do you do? Fix one: ask, "Expensive compared to what?" Their answer shows what you are really competing with.

Fix two: break it down. Sixty pounds a month sounds big. Two pounds a day sounds small. Less than a coffee.

Fix three: show the cost of doing nothing. Ask: "How much is this problem costing you today?"

Golden rule: never cut the price. Add value instead. A bonus, a guarantee, or a smaller package.

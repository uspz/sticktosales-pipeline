Three phrases that kill a sale. And I bet you've said at least one of them this week.

Number one: "Trust me." The moment you say it, people trust you less. Trust is earned, never requested.

Show proof instead. A review, a real result, a photo of a happy customer. Proof speaks louder than promises.

Number two: "It's the best on the market." Everyone says that, so nobody believes it.

Be specific instead. Say: "This saves you two hours every single day." Numbers feel real, and real feels safe.

Number three: "Can I help you?" It is far too easy to answer: "No, thanks, just looking."

Ask an open question instead. "What are you looking for today?" It starts a real conversation.

So remember: show proof, be specific, and ask open questions. Small changes in words make a big change in sales.

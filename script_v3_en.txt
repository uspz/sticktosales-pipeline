A customer says: "I'll think about it." And you never hear from them again. Do you know why?

Most of the time, "I'll think about it" doesn't mean maybe. It means: "I'm not convinced yet."

Reason one: confusion. If people don't understand your offer, they will never say yes.

Reason two: fear. Nobody wants to waste their money, or regret what they bought.

Reason three: the price feels bigger than the value. Fifty pounds feels cheap when the benefit is worth five hundred.

So what do you do? First, ask: "Which part do you want to think about?" It brings the real objection out into the open.

Second, remove the risk. Offer a guarantee, a free trial or a refund.

Third, make the next step small. Instead of "buy now", say: "try it for a week".

That's it for today. Follow for a new sales lesson every day.

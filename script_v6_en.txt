Most sales don't happen on the first message. But most people give up after just one.

Silence rarely means no. Most of the time, it means: "I'm busy", or "I forgot."

The worst follow-up is: "Just checking in." It gives the customer nothing to answer, so it gets ignored.

Fix one: bring value every time. Share a tip, a result, or a useful link.

Fix two: make it easy to answer. Offer two options: "Does Tuesday or Thursday work better for you?"

Fix three: space it out. Follow up after two days, then five, then ten. Persistent, not pushy.

And for the last message, try: "Should I close your file?" People don't like losing an option, so many of them reply.

So remember: add value, make it easy, and be patient. Following up is where many sales are won.

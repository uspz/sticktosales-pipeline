Why do people queue for hours for a limited sneaker drop? Because we want what we might lose. That's scarcity.

Without a deadline, the customer thinks: I'll buy later. And later usually means never.

Tip one: give a real deadline. People act on dates, not on maybe.

Tip two: show what's limited. Ten spots or five units create urgency.

Tip three: show what they lose by waiting. Every month without a website is clients going to your competitor.

Example: a coach opens twenty spots. When twelve are taken, she shares the update, and the rest sell in two days.

One rule: never fake it. A countdown that resets every day kills your credibility.

This week, add one real deadline to your offer, and tell people exactly when it ends.

Would you buy a mattress you've never lain on? You would, if you can send it back for free. That's risk reversal.

Every customer fears one thing: wasting their money. Take that risk on yourself, and the sale gets easy.

Tip one: offer a guarantee. Thirty days, money back, no questions asked.

Tip two: let them try first. A free sample or a small test removes the fear.

Tip three: put it in writing. A promise on paper feels real.

Example: a web designer says: if you don't love the first draft, you pay nothing. Clients stop hesitating, and she closes twice as many.

One rule: only promise what you can keep. A guarantee you dodge destroys trust.

This week, add one clear guarantee to your offer, and say it before the price.

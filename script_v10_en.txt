You trust a restaurant with a line outside more than an empty one. That's social proof, and it sells more than any pitch.

Humans copy humans. When we're unsure, we look at what others chose, and we follow.

Tip one: use specific numbers. Saying you're popular is weak. Two thousand customers this year is proof.

Tip two: tell the story of someone just like your customer. A freelancer, a student, a small shop owner. People believe people who look like them.

Tip three: show the proof at the exact moment of doubt. Put the review right next to the price, not buried on another page.

Example: instead of saying this course is great, say: Ana started with zero clients and signed her first three in one month.

One rule: only use real proof. Fake reviews destroy trust the moment someone spots them.

So this week, collect three real results from your customers, and put them where people decide.
